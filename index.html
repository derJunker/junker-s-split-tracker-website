Level up your Pogostuck Speedrunning experience

A simple overlay for a split tracker with a bunch of new features.

Try it out now!

Features

  • Default Pogostuck Split-Tracker Functionality
    Hover me :)
  • Gold Splits
  • Gold Paces
  • Pace Estimations
  • Sum of Best
  • Reset Counters
  • Custom Modes
  • More Customization (coloring, hide skipped splits, size)
  • Reversed UD Splits

Configure it to your liking.

  • Launch Pogostuck at the same time

    Just open the split-tracker and Pogostuck will open automatically.

  • Adjust Split Colors

    Change any of the used colors: fast, slow, gold splits and even gold pace.

  • Use it as an overlay or on a second monitor

    If you play on fullscreen the overlay is not an option.

    Just set a background and put it on a 2nd monitor (or let it run in the background).

  • Hide features you don't want

    You just want the gold splits but not the rest of the fuss? Sure thing, just hide them in the settings!

  • Old Split Names

    I can't relate, but "some" people like them.

  • Add a Background Color or leave it transparent

    Background is best for 2nd Monitors, transparent for an overlay.
